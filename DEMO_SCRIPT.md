# Demo script: 3–5 minute recording

Every number, ID and quote below comes from the real Phase 2 run artifacts: `cases/*.json`, `runs/cases/<run_id>/trace.json` and `audit.jsonl`. **Do not change any numbers when recording.** If you re-run the pipeline, re-check them against the UI first; run IDs and latencies change on every run, while decisions and graph IDs are deterministic.

The pages are those of the Streamlit UI (`.venv/Scripts/streamlit run ui/app.py`). Customer responses, actions, approvals and reports are **SIMULATED**, and the recording must say so.

**UI layout.**
- The top of the page has the SIMULATED ENVIRONMENT banner, the workflow steps, and KPI cards for verdict, fraud probability, model risk score, approval route and evidence items.
- Section 2 has three separate tabs: **Graph calls**, **Evidence** and **Entity graph**. Only the selected tab is visible, so click a tab to show it.
- There is no confidence card, because the answer files have no confidence field. Don't describe the fraud probability as "confidence".

**Honesty notes for the narration (do not skip):**
- The verdicts are 8 fraud, 11 uncertain and 1 legitimate. That is far from the README's "half the cases are legitimate". Most uncertain cases come from a design threshold for simulated "no reply" responses that is still under review.
- The scorer weights come from a sign-constrained fit. The unconstrained fit scored higher on the backtest, but it contradicted the README's evidence directions, so we rejected it (see `config.py` and `runs/backtest_v1/`).
- The embeddings are lexical (hashed TF-IDF), not neural.
